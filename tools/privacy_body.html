What I collect

Information you provide. You give me information when you book A Sounding, inquire about The Build, express interest in a retreat, or subscribe to The Letters. That typically includes your name, email address, phone number, and details about your business or your reason for reaching out. I use it to respond to you and to deliver what you asked for. Nothing else.

Payment information. Payments are processed through HoneyBook. I do not store your card details. HoneyBook handles that data under its own privacy practices, which you can review at honeybook.com/privacy.

Information collected automatically, and only if you allow it. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics collects general location, device type, browser, pages visited, and time on page. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. I use it to understand what is working on the site. If you decline, none of it is collected and the analytics code never loads.

Health information, retreats only. If you register for a retreat, you may be asked to disclose health conditions, dietary restrictions, physical limitations, or other information relevant to your safety and comfort. This is voluntary. It is held in confidence, shared only with facilitators or vendors directly involved in your care during that retreat, and used for no other purpose.

No health information is collected for A Sounding or for a Build engagement.

Cookies

This site uses cookies, which are small text files stored by your browser, for one thing only: analytics. Nothing is set unless you say yes. The first time you visit you are asked, and until you answer, no analytics code is loaded and no request is made to Google at all. Declining costs you nothing here — every page works exactly the same either way.

Your answer is remembered in your own browser for a year, and then you are asked again. You can change your mind whenever you like:

If you accept, the only cookies set are Google Analytics: _ga and _ga_KDB3GWPNHC. They tell one visit apart from another and expire two years after your last one. There are no advertising cookies here, nothing is shared with an ad network, and nothing on this site follows you onto another one.

Email

If you subscribe to The Letters, your name and email address are stored and managed in HoneyBook or Flodesk, depending on the form you used. I use them to send what you signed up for. You can unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any email. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing.

Affiliate relationships

I am an active wellness partner with MakeWellness. If you purchase through a link I share, I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. I only share products I use. The relationship does not change what I recommend.

Retreats

Where this page and your signed retreat guest contract differ, your signed contract governs.

What a retreat is. Retreats are designed for rest, perspective, and time with a small group. Experiences vary by person. Retreat activities may include movement, workshops, group conversation, outdoor excursions, and cultural experiences, and participation carries inherent risk. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not a licensed medical provider or mental health professional, and nothing that happens on a retreat is clinical treatment.

Booking and confirmation. Your space is reserved when a non refundable deposit is received and Cydnie Jocelyn LLC issues a booking confirmation. Submitting an inquiry or interest form does not hold a space. Spaces are filled in the order deposits are received.

Retreats are capped at fifteen guests. A retreat requires a minimum of eight confirmed guests to run. If a retreat does not reach that minimum by the date stated in your retreat contract, it will be canceled and the terms below for a cancellation by Cydnie Jocelyn LLC apply.

Payment. A non refundable deposit of $500 secures your space. The balance is due by the date in your retreat contract. Payment plans may be available on the terms set at booking. A missed installment may result in cancellation and forfeiture of everything paid to date. Accepted payment methods appear on your HoneyBook invoice. All prices are in U.S. dollars.

Refunds. All payments for retreats are non refundable, including the deposit. Spaces are limited, venue and vendor commitments are made far in advance, and holding your space keeps another guest from taking it.

No refunds are issued for any reason, including illness, emergency, schedule change, or inability to travel. No partial refunds are issued for late arrival, early departure, or activities you choose not to attend. Bookings are not transferable to another person without my written approval.

If I cancel. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC may cancel, postpone, or reschedule a retreat for reasons including low enrollment, safety, venue failure, natural disaster, government restriction, or other events outside my control.

If that happens, you receive retreat credit equal to everything you have paid, valid for two years toward a future retreat. Credits are not redeemable for cash. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not responsible for costs you booked independently, including airfare, separate lodging, transportation, or visa fees.

Travel insurance. Travel insurance is highly recommended for every retreat guest and especially for international retreats. A "cancel for any reason" policy is the only thing that protects your money if you cannot travel, because retreat payments are not refundable.

Before the retreat, every guest completes a travel insurance form. On it you either confirm you have purchased coverage or state that you are declining coverage and accepting the financial risk yourself. The form is required. The coverage is your decision. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not responsible for losses you incur after declining coverage.

Travel documents. For international retreats you are responsible for holding a passport valid at least six months past your return date, obtaining any required visas or entry documents, and meeting every entry requirement of the destination country. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not liable if you are denied entry. Flights are not included unless the retreat description states otherwise, and I do not book or pay for flights on your behalf.

Health and medical disclosure. By booking, you confirm you are physically, mentally, and emotionally able to participate. You agree to tell me in writing about medical conditions, injuries, physical limitations, dietary needs, or health concerns before the retreat begins. That information stays confidential and is used only for your safety and comfort. Consult your physician before attending. I may restrict participation in a specific activity if a condition poses a risk to you or to others.

I cannot guarantee an allergen free environment, particularly abroad where food is prepared by third party vendors. You are responsible for managing your own dietary needs. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not liable for allergic or adverse reactions.

The following may result in removal from a retreat without refund: harassment or discrimination of any kind, behavior that disrupts the safety or well being of the group, use of alcohol or substances in a way that affects other guests, and any conduct I judge unsafe or harmful. I may refuse a booking or remove a participant at any time on these grounds.
