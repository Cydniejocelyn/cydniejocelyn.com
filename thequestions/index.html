You picked one.

There were twelve on the table. Here are the other eleven. Take them with you and use them on someone this week.

I ask questions like these because most rooms don’t.

We get asked what we do and where we work and how the kids are, and we answer all of it without ever saying anything true.

The questions

  1. 01What made you laugh this week?
  2. 02What’s a compliment you’ve never forgotten?
  3. 03Where were you the last time you felt completely at ease?
  4. 04What’s one small thing that made this week better?
  5. 05What do people come to you for?
  6. 06What are you better at now than you were a year ago?
  7. 07What did you love doing when you were ten?
  8. 08Who would be proud of where you are right now?
  9. 09What are you building that no one has seen yet?
  10. 10What would you do this year if it didn’t have to be impressive?
  11. 11What would you say yes to if you knew it would work?
  12. 12Where do you want to go that you haven’t been?

Who’s asking

I’m Cydnie. I’m a brand strategist and consultant in Forest Lake, Minnesota. I work with women who are running something real and carrying most of it by themselves.

By the time someone gets to me, the business usually works. What’s stopped working is her ability to see it clearly from underneath everything sitting on top of it.
