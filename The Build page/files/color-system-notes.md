# Color system notes
### the resurfacing business, site wide

Written as roles rather than hex values, so the Cydnie Jocelyn revamp can change
the values without breaking the logic.

---

## The problem

Home is dark. About is dark. The Build was dark in two places.

If most pages are dark, dark is no longer emphasis. It is the default, and nothing
on the site can be emphasised any more. The Deepwater band on A Sounding was doing
real work when it was rare. Across a site where dark is everywhere, it does nothing.

That is the actual issue, not whether any single page is too dark.

---

## The principle

The brand thesis is that she is not lacking anything, she is under something.

Colour should carry that. Depth is a variable, not a setting. A page should move
from deep toward surface, because that is the direction the work goes. A site that
sits in the deep the whole way through says the opposite of what the copy says.

So: dark marks the moment the condition is named. Light is where the answer lives.
The page resurfaces as the reader scrolls.

---

## The rule

**One Deepwater band per page. No exceptions.**

Everything else runs on Surface, Held, and Held Lift, with Fathom available as the
mid value when a section needs separation but not emphasis.

Rough target ratio across any page:

- 70 percent Surface and Held, the reading environment
- 20 percent Fathom, sections that need to sit apart
- 10 percent Deepwater, used once

---

## Roles

| Role | Token | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Page ground | Surface | Default background for all reading |
| Alternate ground | Held Lift | Adjacent bands, so sections separate without changing value |
| Section separation | Held | Rules, borders, image frames |
| Mid depth | Fathom | Body text on light. Bands that need weight but not emphasis |
| Maximum depth | Deepwater | One band per page. Headline text on light |
| The line | Meniscus | The waterline itself. Eyebrows, labels, numerals |
| Text on depth | Breath | All body copy on Fathom or Deepwater |
| Tertiary | Silt | Captions, footnotes, footer |

---

## Page by page

**Home.** Deepwater hero only, where the condition is named. Every band after it
resurfaces. The last band before the footer should be the lightest on the page.
This is the one place the movement is the whole point, so build it as a gradient
of bands rather than a colour gradient. No gradient fills anywhere.

**About.** Should be light throughout. This is the longest reading on the site,
it is prose only, and the faith passage is testimony. Testimony is quiet. Dark
grounds add weight the passage does not want, and long-form serif at display
weights loses stroke on dark. If one band goes dark, it is a short one, and not
the faith section.

**The Build.** Deepwater on A Sounding. That is the one action on the page.
The full engagement moved to Fathom in v6, because putting the heaviest value on
the most expensive item reads as pressure.

**Retreats.** The defensible exception. Dark reads as evening and away rather
than as weight. If any page runs deeper than the others, this is the one.

**The Letters.** Light. It is reading.

---

## Contrast floor

Body copy on any dark band uses Breath, not Meniscus. Meniscus is for the
waterline, labels, and numerals only. It does not have the contrast to carry
a paragraph.

Check every text and ground pair at 4.5:1 minimum before launch. Display type
at large sizes can go to 3:1, body copy cannot.

---

## For the revamp

**The palette is not the problem.** Fathom, Deepwater, Breath, Meniscus and the
neutrals are a coherent system. What needs fixing is distribution, not values.
Do not let the revamp turn into a repalette.

**The old brand board is retired.** Teal and cream, script wordmark, "Enter The
Ecosystem." It cannot appear on this site, and it is why her own board is not in
the proof block. It needs rebuilding in the new system.

**One thing to carry forward.** The water caustics image on the old board. The
metaphor was already there before it was named.

**One thing to check.** Caustics are ripples, and the brand guide says the
waterline is always level and never a wave. If water imagery returns, it is
still water, and it is still not animated in a loop.

**Confirm the hex values.** The wireframes are running placeholders. Surface in
particular is a cool grey-green in the file, and if the real value is lighter,
some of the heaviness on screen is the placeholder rather than the structure.
