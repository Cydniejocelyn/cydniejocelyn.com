Why this exists

You are not tired because something is wrong with you.

You are the one everything runs through. The business, the house, the calendar, the people. It works because you are holding it, and holding it is the part nobody sees.

Eight days will not fix that. What it does is put you somewhere the weight is not yours for a week, with women in the same position, and let you find out what you think when nothing is asking for you.

The week

Built for the Tuesday after.

Mornings start with movement led by Kris Krause. Strength, mobility, and whatever your body will actually do that day. It is not a class you can be behind in.

Days alternate. Some are given over to Crete: the gorge trails, the harbour at Chania, the villages that have been there since the eighteenth century. Others are yours entirely, with nothing on them, and no one keeping track of where you went.

There are two sittings across the week where the group works through something together. What they are goes out with the schedule once you have a seat. No workbook, no binder, and nothing you have to report back on.

Evenings are dinner. The first night and the last night are both dinners with the full group. In between, some nights that happens and some nights it does not.

The excursion lineup is still being finalised. Everyone holding a seat gets the full schedule as it lands.

Who this is not for.

If you want a mastermind, this is not one. There is no curriculum, no framework, and no deck.

If you need the week to produce something measurable, do not book it. You will spend eight days auditing the experience instead of having it.

If you cannot be unreachable for a stretch of days, book a year from now instead. Crete is nine hours ahead and the point of it is the distance.

I run diagnostic work and I am happy to talk business while we are there. That is not what the week is for, and it is not what you are paying for.

The place

Douliana, Crete.

Douliana sits a hundred metres above sea level in the Apokoronas region of western Crete, on a hilltop above Souda Bay. About forty houses. Stone streets, oaks and cypress at the entrance, and an architecture that has not changed much since the eighteenth century.

It is a village people live in rather than a resort town. That is why it was chosen.

Region

Apokoronas, western Crete. Traditional villages, mountains behind, water in front.

The sea

Almirida, Kalyves, and Kera Beach are about fifteen minutes away. Kera is the quiet one and the last stretch is a forty minute walk in.

Walking

Trails leave from the village through gorges and woodland and end at Agios Ioannis, a church built into a cave.

Nearby

Chania old town and harbour, folklore museums, and the villages in between.

Airport

Chania International (CHQ), about 33 km. Group transport to the property is included both ways.

Where you stay

Armonia Retreat Center.

Four thousand square metres of grounds in the middle of the village. Indoor and outdoor gathering space, a full kitchen, a lounge, a gym, a pool, and a sauna.

Fifteen guests, two to a room, twin beds. Kris and I are in the house as well, which makes seventeen women on the property for the week. Every room has its own bathroom, air conditioning, linens, and towels. If you are travelling with someone, say so when you book and you will be roomed together.

Before you ask

Questions

Do I have to know someone going?
Most women come alone. That is the normal way to do this, not the brave version of it. Fifteen is small enough that by the second day you are not on the edge of anything.
What fitness level does this require?
None in particular. Kris designs the movement around who is in the room. Some of the walking in Crete is on uneven ground and there are hills, so if stairs and trails are a real concern, tell me before you book and I will be straight with you about what the week involves.
Is this a work thing or a rest thing?
Rest. There is no deliverable and nothing to report back on. I am a consultant and I do run diagnostic work, but that is A Sounding and it is a separate thing you can book any time.
Can I come with a friend?
Yes, and you will be roomed together. Say so when you book. It does not change the price either way.
Can you accommodate how I eat?
Yes. List anything when you book and it gets to the kitchen well before you land. Meals are cooked on site with Cretan ingredients, so most things are straightforward.
Do I need travel insurance?
It is not required and I would buy it anyway. The deposit is non refundable and your flight will be booked months out. Insurance is the only thing covering either of those.
What if I have a question you have not answered here?
Email me. A question you are sitting on for three weeks is worth two minutes of my time to answer.
