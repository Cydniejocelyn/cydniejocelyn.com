The format

Fifteen.

Every retreat is capped at fifteen women. Not because it sells better that way. Because fifteen is how many people can actually be known in a week.

The days hold rest, movement, food somebody else made, and long stretches of unscheduled time. There are conversations, and they are real ones. There is no workbook, no morning intention circle, and no expectation that you arrive with anything worked out.

You do not need to prepare. That is true of everything I run.

Who this is not for.

Three kinds of week this is not, and then the one it is.

If you want a mastermind, this is the wrong week. Nobody is going to pitch, and there is no room set aside for it.

If you want a curriculum and a binder to take home, I do not make those. The value here is not portable in that form.

If you need the week to produce a measurable outcome before you can justify the cost, do not book it. You will spend the whole of it auditing the experience instead of having it, and that is a bad use of your money.

What is left is a woman who is tired of being the one everybody depends on and wants a stretch of days where that is not the arrangement.

What it holds

Built for the week after.

That woman is the one the week is built around, so here is what it actually holds.

A retreat that only works while you are on it is a vacation. The point is the Tuesday you get home and something is different about how you answer the phone.

The structure is loose on purpose. Enough shape that the days do not drift, enough air that you can leave the room. Nobody will notice out loud if you sleep through the morning.

You will come home knowing fourteen women well enough to text them. That part is not designed. It happens every time.

Before you book

What you are agreeing to, and what people ask.

The terms first, because they are the part that should never arrive as a surprise. Then the questions, in the order they actually turn up.

Three things you are agreeing to, and none of them changes after you book.

Deposit
$500 holds your seat. It comes off the total, and it is not refundable.
Balance
Pay in full, or in six monthly payments. If neither of those works, say so and we will talk about it.
Travel insurance
Buy it, and buy it the day you book. Your deposit and your flights are both committed early. Insurance covers the things I cannot.

Most of the rest comes down to one worry, which is whether you would be the wrong person in the room.

Do I have to come with someone?

Most women come alone. That is the normal way to do this, not the brave version of it. Fifteen is small enough that by the second day you are not on the edge of anything.

Is this a work thing or a rest thing?

Rest. There is no curriculum, no deliverable, and nothing you have to report back on. I am a consultant and I do run diagnostic work, but that is A Sounding and it is a separate thing you can book any time.

What fitness level does this require?

None in particular. Movement is led by Kris Krause and designed around who is in the room, not around who is fittest. Where the walking is on uneven ground it says so on that retreat’s page, and if stairs and trails are a real concern, tell me before you book and I will be straight with you.

Can you accommodate how I eat?

Yes. List anything when you book and it reaches the kitchen well before you land.

The rest is arithmetic and dates, and all of it is published rather than sent after a call.

What does it cost and how do I hold a seat?

Greece is $3,265 at the early rate through 6 September 2026 and $3,450 after that, with a $500 non-refundable deposit. April 2027 is not priced yet; when it is, the letters hear first. Every figure is published on the retreat’s own page.

Do I need travel insurance?

It is not required and I would buy it anyway, on the day you book. The deposit is non-refundable and your flight will be booked months out. Insurance is the only thing covering either of those.

Can I book a private retreat for my own group?

Yes. Small groups, teams and communities. Send an inquiry and we will start with what you are actually trying to give them.
