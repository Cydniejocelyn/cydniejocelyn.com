Why this exists

You are not lacking anything. You are under something.

Most of the women who book a Sounding can list what they have built. Revenue. A client roster. A team, or the beginning of one. A reputation that brings work in without being asked. What they cannot do is find the thing that makes all of it heavier every month.

That thing is almost always structural. It is rarely a mindset and it is almost never located where it is felt. The bookkeeping is late because the pricing is wrong. The work is exhausting because you are still the only person who can do the part that matters.

Ninety minutes with someone outside the business is usually enough to find it.

The ninety minutes

One conversation, across the whole business

You talk. I ask questions and take notes. We move across the whole business rather than one department of it, because pressure travels and the place it lands is rarely the place it starts.

Ninety minutes is the length, not an estimate. A conversation with no end does not produce a decision.

Nothing to prepare
There is no intake form and no pre-work. If you can describe your last two weeks, you have everything you need.
Nothing to organise
You bring the tangle in whatever order it comes out. Sorting it is the work, not the prerequisite.
Remote or in person
Remote by default. In person around the Twin Cities metro and Forest Lake, Minnesota, by arrangement.
Nothing waiting at the end
No second session is scheduled after it. If you want to keep working together, you say so.

Two days later

You leave with a page you can act on

Two days after we talk, you get one written page by email. It says what I heard, what is load bearing, and what I would do first.

It is written to be used. You can act on every line of it without me, and you can hand it to a partner or a spouse to explain what you just spent money on.

It is yours whether or not we ever work together again.

What the page is not

It is not a report and it is not a scored assessment. The length is a page because a page gets read and a deck gets saved for later.

The part most people leave out

Sometimes the answer is that you don’t need me.

Sometimes the page says the move is a hire. Sometimes it says the move is a price change, or a contract you already have sitting unsigned, or nothing at all for a quarter while something else settles. None of that is work I would bill you for.
