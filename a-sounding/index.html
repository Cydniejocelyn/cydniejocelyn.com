The part most people leave out

Sometimes the answer is that you don’t need me.

Sometimes the page says the move is a hire. Sometimes a price change, a contract already sitting unsigned, or nothing at all for a quarter while something else settles. None of that is work I would bill you for, and I will tell you when it is the answer.

Hearing it in week one is worth more than $300. The alternative is a year spent solving the wrong problem.

Before you book

Questions people ask first.

Is this a coaching call?

No. There is no goal setting and nothing to work on between sessions. It is a diagnostic. You describe how the business currently runs, and you leave with the pressure located and a page naming what to address first.

Does the $300 apply toward a larger engagement?

Yes. If you go on to a Build, the $300 comes off it. A Sounding is still complete on its own: the page is yours either way, and whether to continue is a decision you make after it is in your hands.

What if I do not know what my problem is?

That is the usual starting point. Locating it is the work of the ninety minutes, and it is the reason the conversation covers the whole business rather than the part you think is broken.

What if I am not ready to spend $300?

Then ask me a question or start with the letters. They are free, they arrive once a week, and they are written the week they go out. Read the letters.
