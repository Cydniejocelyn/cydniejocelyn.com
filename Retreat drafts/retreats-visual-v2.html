The format

Fifteen

Every retreat is capped at fifteen women. Not because it sells better that way. Because past fifteen, a room stops being a group and becomes an audience, and you go home having met nobody.

The days hold rest, movement, food that somebody else made, and long stretches of unscheduled time. There are conversations, and they are real ones. There is no workbook, no morning intention circle, and no expectation that you arrive with anything worked out.

You do not need to prepare. That is true of everything I run.

Who this is not for.

If you want a mastermind, this is the wrong week. Nobody is going to pitch, and there is no room set aside for it.

If you want a curriculum and a binder to take home, I do not make those. The value here is not portable in that form.

If you need the week to produce a measurable outcome before you can justify the cost, do not book it. You will spend eight days auditing the experience instead of having it, and that is a bad use of your money.

What is left is a woman who is tired of being the one everyone depends on and wants seven days where that is not the arrangement.

What it holds

Built for the week after.

A retreat that only works while you are on it is a vacation. The point is the Tuesday in May when you get home and something is different about how you answer the phone.

The structure is loose on purpose. Enough shape that the days do not drift, enough air that you can leave the room. Nobody will notice out loud if you sleep through the morning.

You will come home knowing fourteen women well enough to text them. That part is not designed. It happens every time.

Before you ask

Questions

01 Do I have to come with someone?
Most people come alone. That is the normal way to do this, not the brave exception. Fifteen is small enough that you will not be on the edge of anything by the second day.
02 Is this a work thing or a rest thing?
Rest. There is no curriculum, no deliverable, and nothing you have to report back on. I am a consultant and I do run diagnostic work, but that is A Sounding and it is a different thing entirely. If you want to talk business while you are there, we will. If you want to sit by the water for eight days, that is also the whole point.
03 What does it cost and how do I hold a seat?
Greece is priced and open, and the figure and deposit terms are on the Greece page. April is not priced yet. When it is, the list hears first.
04 Can I book a private retreat for my group?
Yes. Small groups, teams, and communities. Send an inquiry and we will start with what you are actually trying to give them.
