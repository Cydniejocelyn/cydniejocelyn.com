Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not a licensed medical provider or mental health professional, and nothing that happens on a retreat is clinical treatment.

**Booking and confirmation.**
Your space is reserved when a non refundable deposit is received and Cydnie Jocelyn LLC issues a booking confirmation. Submitting an inquiry or interest form does not hold a space. Spaces are filled in the order deposits are received.

Retreats are capped at fifteen guests. A retreat requires a minimum of eight confirmed guests to run. If a retreat does not reach that minimum by the date stated in your retreat contract, it will be canceled and the terms below for a cancellation by Cydnie Jocelyn LLC apply.

**Payment.**
A non refundable deposit of $500 secures your space. The balance is due by the date in your retreat contract. Payment plans may be available on the terms set at booking. A missed installment may result in cancellation and forfeiture of everything paid to date. Accepted payment methods appear on your HoneyBook invoice. All prices are in U.S. dollars.

**Refunds.**
All payments for retreats are non refundable, including the deposit. Spaces are limited, venue and vendor commitments are made far in advance, and holding your space keeps another guest from taking it.

Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not responsible for costs you booked independently, including airfare, separate lodging, transportation, or visa fees.

**Travel insurance.**
Travel insurance is highly recommended for every retreat guest and especially for international retreats. A "cancel for any reason" policy is the only thing that protects your money if you cannot travel, because retreat payments are not refundable.

Before the retreat, every guest completes a travel insurance form. On it you either confirm you have purchased coverage or state that you are declining coverage and accepting the financial risk yourself. The form is required. The coverage is your decision. Cydnie Jocelyn LLC is not responsible for losses you incur after declining coverage.
