02 Why this exists

You are not lacking anything. You are under something.

Most of the women who book a Sounding can list what they have built. Revenue. A client roster. A team, or the beginning of one. A reputation that brings work in without being asked. What they cannot do is find the thing that makes all of it heavier every month.

That thing is almost always structural. It is rarely a mindset, and it is almost never located where it is felt. The bookkeeping is late because the pricing is wrong. The work is exhausting because you are still the only person who can do the part that matters.

Ninety minutes with someone outside the business is usually enough to find it.

Thesis block placed second, not first. The hero sells the mechanics because that is what a cold visitor needs. This block does the recognition work for the visitor who arrived from social and already knows the language.

The examples are concrete on purpose. Generic pain copy is the coaching category's signature. Naming late bookkeeping and a bottlenecked owner tells her you have seen a real business rather than a persona.

03 The ninety minutes

One conversation about the whole business

You talk. I ask questions and take notes. We move across the whole business rather than one department of it, because pressure travels and the place it lands is rarely the place it starts.

Ninety minutes is the length, not an estimate. A conversation with no end does not produce a decision.

  • Nothing to prepareThere is no intake form and no pre-work. If you can describe your last two weeks, you have everything you need.
  • Nothing to organise firstYou bring the tangle in whatever order it comes out. Sorting it is the work, not the prerequisite.
  • Remote or in personRemote by default. In person in the Twin Cities metro and around Forest Lake by arrangement.
  • No second session waiting at the endNothing is scheduled after. If you want to keep working together, you say so.

"No preparation" is a brand-wide principle, so it leads the list. Every other diagnostic offer in this category opens with a questionnaire. Yours opens with nothing, and that is the differentiator most likely to be believed because it is checkable at booking.

Location signal lives inside real copy. Forest Lake and Twin Cities appear in a line a human would actually read, which is what the local schema needs to be corroborated by.

Open item for you. If in person has a travel radius or a surcharge, it belongs here rather than surfacing in the confirmation email.

04 Two days later

You leave with a page you can act on

Two days after we talk, you get one written page by email. It says what I heard, what is load bearing, and what I would do first.

It is written to be used. You can act on every line of it without me, and you can hand it to a partner or a spouse to explain what you just spent money on.

It is yours whether or not we ever work together again.

What the page is not

It is not a report and it is not a scored assessment. Length is a page because a page gets read and a deck gets saved for later.

06 The part most people leave out

Sometimes the answer is that you don't need me.

Sometimes the page says the move is a hire. Sometimes it says the move is a price change, or a contract you already have sitting unsigned, or nothing at all for a quarter while something else settles. None of that is work I would bill you for.

I will tell you when that is the answer. Hearing it in week one is worth more than $300, because the alternative is a year spent solving the wrong problem.

Cut: refund clause. That is handled in the conversation, not published on the page. The differentiation statement carries the block on its own.

No invented numbers here. The temptation is to add "this has only happened twice." Do not, unless it is true and you would say the number out loud.

08 Before you book

Questions people ask first

Is this a coaching call?

No. There is no goal setting and nothing to work on between sessions. It is a diagnostic. You describe how the business currently runs, and you leave with the pressure located and a page naming what to address first.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. There is no intake form and no pre-work. If you can describe your last two weeks, you have everything you need.

Does the $300 apply toward a larger engagement?

No. A Sounding is the first paid step and it is complete on its own. It is not a deposit. If you continue into a build, that is a separate decision made after the written page is in your hands.

What if I do not know what my problem is?

That is the usual starting point. Locating it is the work of the ninety minutes, and it is the reason the conversation covers the whole business rather than the part you think is broken.

Is it in person or remote?

Remote by default. In person is available in the Twin Cities metro and around Forest Lake by arrangement.

What happens after?

Two days later the page arrives by email. Nothing else is scheduled and there is no follow up sequence. If you want to keep working together, you say so.

Six entries, all mirrored in FAQPage schema. Three of them are objections rather than questions: the coaching one, the credit one, and the one about not knowing the problem. Those are the ones doing conversion work.

"What happens after" doubles as the anti-funnel signal. Saying there is no follow up sequence is checkable, which makes the rest of the page more believable.
