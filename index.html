Questions

Before you ask.

Something not here? Ask me directly. I answer every one myself.

What does Cydnie Jocelyn do?

Cydnie Jocelyn is a brand and business strategist. She partners with founders and leaders on brand identity, websites, operations and business development, and hosts small-group retreats for women.

How much does it cost to work with you?

Pricing is published. A one-day strategy intensive is $1,500, a season of partnership starts at $6,000, websites at $4,000, content at $3,600 a quarter, and a full brand launch at $15,000. Most partnerships begin with a free 30-minute call, and the $300 Sounding comes off your project.

Do you work with clients outside Minnesota?

Yes. I’m based in Forest Lake, Minnesota and work remotely with founders and leaders across the United States, and in person around Minneapolis and Saint Paul.

How is a partnership different from hiring an agency or a freelancer?

An agency delivers a project and moves on. I stay in it with you across brand, operations and growth, as one person who knows the whole business, because I only succeed when you do.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute call. If it’s a fit, you start with a Sounding: 90 minutes on your business and a written roadmap of findings and recommendations two days later, with the $300 fee credited to your project.

Who are the retreats for?

Women who hold everything together and need a few days to set it down. You don’t need to own a business to come. The next retreat is in Gatlinburg, Tennessee, 13 to 18 April 2027.
