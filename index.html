The condition

It is 11:40pm and the tabs in your mind won't close.

  1. 01

    You are not tired from work. You are tired from being continuously required.

  2. 02

    You have run out of external explanations. So the fault has quietly gone internal.

  3. 03

    You are not stuck because you don't know what to do. You are stuck because you are waiting to be sure you will do it right.

  4. 04

    You have been sold vibes before. You paid for a shift and went home with a journal and no plan.

  5. 05

    You have not had an unclaimed hour in years. That frightens you more than the exhaustion does.

You didn't lose yourself.
You got buried under
everyone who needed you.

Everything else in this category tells you that you have potential. You have a résumé full of evidence that you are capable, so flattery bounces off. What nothing has explained is why capable feels like this.

Water explains it. You are not lacking anything and you are not behind. You are under something, and pressure is not a character flaw.

Nothing is wrong with you. Something is on top of you. Those are completely different problems, and only one of them is yours to fix.

Why me

I was the client.

Not as a story. As a timeline.

  1. Three years of being bullied and targeted at a company where that was not unusual for women. Postpartum. Motherhood. And hospitals, because my children have a rare genetic mutation and we have not stopped going since.
  2. I had never taken a last-minute trip in my life. The invitation came and I said yes in three days, with no plan, after three years of not moving.
  3. I was alone in a group of fifteen, and I heard myself for the first time in years.
  4. That is the week I resurfaced. I came back and told that company I had a choice after all.

I am not describing a woman I once coached. I lead with heart and close with operations. Heart alone is a nice conversation. Operations alone is why you are tired.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is this brand strategy, or is this a conversation about how I feel?

It is brand strategy, and it starts with how you feel because that is where the information is. You cannot position a business when the person running it cannot say plainly what she wants it to do. So I lead with heart and I close with operations. You will leave with words for your business, not a mood. And if what you actually need is a therapist rather than a strategist, I will say so in the first twenty minutes rather than the last.

What if I cannot explain what is wrong?

That is the normal starting condition, and it is the thing being solved. You have run out of external explanations, so the fault has quietly gone internal, and that is very hard to describe out loud. Bring it in the state it is in.

What actually happens, and when?

We talk once, for as long as it takes, and I listen rather than wait for my turn. Two days later a written page arrives: what you said, in the order it actually goes, and one clear next thing to do. That page is yours whether or not we ever speak again.

What if I am not ready to spend $300?

Then start with the twelve questions. They are free, they arrive one a month, and they are the same twelve I send retreat guests before they fly. Several people have answered a few of them and worked out on their own what they were going to hire me to help them see. That is a fine outcome. Send me the twelve questions.
