Why me

I was the client.

Not as a story. As a timeline.

  1. Three years of being bullied and targeted at a company where that was not unusual for women. Postpartum. Motherhood. And hospitals, because my children have a rare genetic mutation and we have not stopped going since.
  2. I had never taken a last-minute trip in my life. The invitation came and I said yes in three days, with no plan, after two and a half years of not moving.
  3. I was alone in a group of fifteen, and I heard myself for the first time in years.
  4. That is the week I resurfaced. I came back and told that company I had a choice after all.

I am not describing a woman I once coached. I lead with heart and close with operations. Heart alone is a nice conversation. Operations alone is why you are tired.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is this brand strategy, or is this a conversation about how I feel?

It is brand strategy, and it starts with how you feel because that is where the information is. You cannot position a business when the person running it cannot say plainly what she wants it to do. So I lead with heart and I close with operations. You will leave with words for your business, not a mood.

What if I cannot explain what is wrong?

That is the normal starting condition, and it is the thing being solved. You have run out of external explanations, so the fault has quietly gone internal, and that is very hard to describe out loud. Bring it in the state it is in.

What actually happens, and when?

We talk once, for ninety minutes, and I ask a lot of questions because the useful information is not in the pitch deck. Two days later the written analysis arrives. It is yours to keep either way.

What if I am not ready to spend $300?

Worth knowing first: if you go on to a Build, the $300 comes off it, so it is not money spent twice. If that is still not the right moment, ask me a question or start with the letters. They are free, they arrive once a week, and they are written the week they go out. Read the letters.
