Zone 3 · The twelve

The questions

  1. 01What made you laugh this week?
  2. 02What's a compliment you've never forgotten?
  3. 03Where were you the last time you felt completely at ease?
  4. 04What's one small thing that made this week better?
  5. 05What do people come to you for?
  6. 06What are you better at now than you were a year ago?
  7. 07What did you love doing when you were ten?
  8. 08Who would be proud of where you are right now?
  9. 09What are you building that no one has seen yet?
  10. 10What would you do this year if it didn't have to be impressive?
  11. 11What would you say yes to if you knew it would work?
  12. 12Where do you want to go that you haven't been?
Fixes carried in from the live page:
· Question 11 currently reads "what would you say yest to." Typo.
· Casing is inconsistent on the live page (wHERE, wHO, wHAT). Sentence case throughout here.
· Order is reversed on the live page, 05 down to 01 then 12 down to 06. Runs 01 to 12 here so a person holding card 07 can find 07.

Numbering is functional, not decorative. The cards on the table are numbered, so the numerals are how someone locates the one in their hand. Keep them.

Structure: one flat list, hairline rules, no cards, no columns, no reveal on scroll. Twelve rows is a long scroll on a phone and that is fine. The scroll is the experience.

Zone 6 · Quiet second door

If $300 isn't where you are tonight, I write letters. You can get those here.

Text link, never a button. That is the whole difference between a second door and a second ask.

Points at The Letters signup on the main site, so no Flodesk embed on this page and no second form to maintain.

Copy variants for the line, pick one:
A. If $300 isn't where you are tonight, I write letters. You can get those here.
B. I also write letters. They're here.
C. There's a letter that goes out from me. You can get it here.

A names the price gap out loud, which is honest but puts money in the last thing she reads. B is the flattest and does not mention the price at all. I'd take B unless you want the page to work harder.
