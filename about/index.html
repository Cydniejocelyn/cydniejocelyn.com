About Cydnie

I’m Cydnie.

I spent fourteen years performing a strength I did not have. Most of what I do now comes out of that.

Most of the women I talk to are doing fine on paper.

The business works. They are the only reason it works, and they have been the only reason for a long time.

Nobody around them can see a problem, so at some point they stopped bringing it up.

I came home and I quit.

For fourteen years I performed a strength I did not have. I was competent and respected and completely hollow, and I could not say that out loud anywhere, least of all at work.

I missed the first two and a half years of my firstborn's life inside postpartum depression, giving everything I had to a company that taught me to devalue myself. That is the sentence it took me years to be able to write.

Then Costa Rica. I decided in three days and the trip left in two weeks, after three years of not moving. I went as a guest. I was alone in a group of fifteen, and I heard myself for the first time in years. Somebody asked me a question I could not answer and I sat in it for the rest of the year.

I came back and told that company I had a choice after all. What I built next, I built while my boys were in and out of hospitals with a rare genetic mutation and nobody was coming to do it for me.

Come up

God isn't an afterthought. He is the whole foundation.

Three things I believe, and where each one came from.

A women's bible study came first. Then church, which I had been avoiding with the specific energy of someone who suspects it will ask something of them. Then baptism, and Brandis Mager, who gave me the courage to go through with it.

You were made on purpose.

I could not have said that about myself out loud. It was true anyway.

The pivots are where God does His best work.

Three days to decide. Two weeks to leave. None of that was a plan.

You were never meant to stay in rooms that required you to be less.

I stayed in one for fourteen years and called it being competent.

The method

Three words, in order.

  1. 01

    Resurface

    Working out what is actually going on. Usually it is not the thing you came in thinking it was.

  2. 02

    Reclaim

    Getting back the hours and the decisions you gave away to keep it running.

  3. 03

    Build

    Setting it up so it does not fall over when you step back.
